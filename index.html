About Me

Hello! I'm Erum Hassan, a Chemical Engineer and Data Scientist passionate about applying computational modeling, data analytics, and sustainability principles to improve chemical processes. I am currently pursuing my Bachelor of Engineering at the University of Oklahoma, specializing in Sustainability, Data Science, and Analytics.

As a Research Intern at the Gallogly College of Engineering, I have worked extensively on Density Functional Theory (DFT) simulations, designing methodologies to assess structural strain effects on IrO₂ surfaces for optimizing the Oxygen Evolution Reaction (OER). My research focuses on electronic structure modifications, Density of States (DOS) analysis, and Bader charge calculations.

I am actively seeking full-time job and internship opportunities where I can apply my skills in computational research, data analytics, and process optimization to drive sustainable and innovative solutions.
